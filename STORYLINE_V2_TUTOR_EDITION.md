# Algebra Adventure: Tutor Edition — Storyline v2

**Working Title:** *Kuya Dex’s Radical Tutorial*  
**Alternative Titles:** *Inverse & Radical: Tutor Quest*, *MathTuto: Math Mission*, *Algebra Adventure: Tutor Road*

---

## Premise

You’ve just moved to a new town, hoping to find work. Money is tight, jobs are scarce, but you’ve always been good at math — especially the topics students struggle with most: **radicals** and **inverse functions**.

You decide to offer tutoring services. At first, you help one neighbor’s kid for free. Word spreads. Soon students, parents, and even schools are asking for your help. As your reputation grows, you move from tutoring in the barangay to helping city students, provincial scholars, regional competition teams, and finally the national math program.

By the end, you’re not just earning a living — you’ve become the tutor every student wants to learn from.

---

## Main Character

**Name:** Customizable by the player. Default: *Kuya Dex* (male) or *Ate Dex* (female).  
**Role:** Math tutor specializing in radicals and inverse functions.  
**Personality:** Patient, encouraging, practical. Explains math using everyday situations.  
**Goal:** Build a successful tutoring career while helping students overcome their fear of radicals and inverse functions.

## Story Arc

### Level 1 — Barangay: First Students
You arrive in town with almost nothing. You post flyers offering tutoring. A parent asks you to help their child who is failing a quiz on square roots. You tutor them for free, and the child passes. Word spreads. By the end of Level 1, you have a small group of barangay students and your first few paying clients.

### Level 2 — City: Building a Reputation
Your barangay students tell their cousins and classmates in the city. You start tutoring high school students preparing for exams. You help with inverse functions in science class, radicals in geometry, and real-world problems like temperature conversion and braking distance. A city school teacher notices your work.

### Level 3 — Province: Scholar Tutor
The provincial scholarship program invites you to help their scholars pass the qualifying exam. The problems get harder: optimizing farm layouts with the distance formula, reversing dosage formulas, solving radical equations in engineering contexts.

### Level 4 — Region: Competition Coach
A regional STEM coordinator hires you to coach students for the Regional Math Competition. Now you’re training top students to solve competition-level radical equations and inverse-function problems under time pressure.

### Level 5 — National: The Olympiad Mentor
Your top student qualifies for the Philippine Math Olympiad training camp. You’re invited to the national level as a guest tutor. The final missions involve the hardest radical and inverse-function problems in the curriculum. You prove that with the right tutor, any student can master math.

---

## Ending

After the national competition, your student earns a medal. You’re offered a permanent position with the national math education program — but you choose to stay in your new town and open a learning center so you can keep helping ordinary students become extraordinary.

**Final message:**
> *“Every student can learn math. Sometimes they just need the right tutor — and that tutor is you.”*

## Math Focus Rationale

The client requested that **every mission focus on radicals or inverse functions**. This is explained in-universe as:

> *“These are the two topics students fear most. That’s why they need a specialist tutor.”*

To keep 50 missions fresh, each mission wraps the same math topic in a different real-world context:

### Radicals contexts
- Pythagorean theorem / diagonal distances
- Distance formula on a map
- Braking distance / physics formulas
- Area and volume formulas that produce square roots
- Signal strength / decay formulas
- Simplifying radical expressions for exams

### Inverse functions contexts
- Unit conversions (temperature, currency, measurement)
- Recipe scaling
- Reverse percentage problems (finding original price)
- Medicine dosage reversal
- Code / cipher decoding
- Undoing formulas to find an input

## Tone and Dialogue Style

- **Warm and encouraging:** The tutor builds students up, never shames them.
- **Practical:** Math is explained through everyday Filipino situations.
- **Bilingual-friendly:** NPCs may use Taglish naturally, e.g., *“Kuya, hindi ko gets ang square root.”*
- **Progressive confidence:** Early students are nervous; later students are eager and competitive.

---

## Level Progression Rules

- Complete all 10 missions in a level to unlock the next level.
- Some missions have prerequisites (e.g., a parent’s referral unlocks a school mission).
- Each mission rewards coins and points based on difficulty.
- Level-up requires a target accuracy and badge count, matching the existing validation system.
