# Mission Remap v2 — Tutor Edition

**Theme:** A new tutor in town helps students master **radicals** and **inverse functions**.  
**Structure:** 5 levels × 10 missions = 50 missions.  
**Prerequisites:** Most missions are sequential; some require completion of earlier missions in the same level.

---
