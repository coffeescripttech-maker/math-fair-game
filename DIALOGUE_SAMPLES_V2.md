# Dialogue Samples v2 — Tutor Edition

**Tone:** Warm, encouraging, practical. Math is explained through everyday Filipino situations.  
**Language:** Mostly English with natural Taglish flavor.

---

## Opening Scene: Arrival in Town

**Setting:** A small barangay. The player character stands near a bulletin board.

**Narration:**
> *You just arrived in town with your bag and a few printed flyers. Rent is due soon, and you need to find work. You’re good at math — especially the parts students always find hard. You decide to post a flyer offering tutoring services.*

**Player thought:**
> *"Okay, let me post this here. Sana may kumatok. I can tutor radicals, inverse functions, algebra...”*

**Aling Maria (Parent, passing by):**
> *"Uy, anak, nagtututor ka pala ng math? My son Miguel is failing his quiz sa square root. Pwede mo ba siyang tulungan? Wala pa kaming pambayad ng malaki, pero kapag pumasa siya, ipapakilala kita sa buong barangay.”*

**Player:**
> *"Sige po, Aling Maria. Tutulungan ko si Miguel. Let’s start with something he sees every day — the basketball court.”*

## Mission 1: Court Diagonal

**NPC:** Miguel (Barangay student)  
**Location:** Basketball Court

**Miguel:**
> *"Kuya/Ate, hindi ko talaga gets ang square root. Bakit may √ sa math?”*

**Player:**
> *"Tingnan mo itong court. Kapag tumatakbo ka from one corner to the opposite corner, hindi ka dumaan sa gilid — diagonal ka. Ang diagonal ng rectangle, pwede mong hanapin gamit ang Pythagorean theorem. Square root ang lalabas.”*

**Miguel:**
> *"So kung alam ko ang length at width, kaya ko nang hanapin ang diagonal?”*

**Player:**
> *"Exactly! Try mo: length = 28m, width = 15m. Find the diagonal.”*

**Quiz prompt:**
> *A barangay basketball court is 28 meters long and 15 meters wide. What is the diagonal distance from one corner to the opposite corner?*
> - Answer: √(28² + 15²) = √1009 ≈ 31.76 meters

**After success:**
> *Miguel:* “Ang galing! Ngayon gets ko na. Salamat, Kuya/Ate!”

## Mission 2: Recipe Scaling

**NPC:** Aling Maria (Parent)  
**Location:** Sari-Sari Store

**Aling Maria:**
> *"Kuya/Ate, may handaan kami bukas. Ang recipe ko good for 8 people, pero 5 lang ang darating. Paano ko babawasan ang ingredients?”*

**Player:**
> *"Ito ang inverse function. Kung ang recipe ay para sa 8, at gusto mo para sa 5, i-multiply mo ang bawat ingredient ng 5/8. Pero kung alam mo lang ang final amount at gusto mong balikan kung ilang tao ang kaya, baliktad ang formula — inverse function.”*

**Aling Maria:**
> *"So parang undo?”*

**Player:**
> *"Yes! Kung ang formula ay amount = (servings) × k, ang inverse ay servings = amount ÷ k.”*

**Quiz prompt:**
> *A recipe calls for 2 cups of flour to serve 8 people. How many cups of flour are needed to serve 5 people? If you used 3 cups of flour, how many people can you serve?*

**After success:**
> *Aling Maria:* “Salamat! Ipapakilala kita sa mga kapitbahay ko.”

## Mission 11: Simplify for the Exam

**NPC:** Carla (High school student)  
**Location:** City Public School

**Carla:**
> *"Kuya/Ate, may exam kami bukas sa radicals. Natatakot ako. Ang daming rules!”*

**Player:**
> *"Relax. Radicals are just reverse exponents. Kung alam mo ang square, alam mo ang square root. Simplify lang natin nang paunti-unti.”*

**Carla:**
> *"Paano kapag may variable? Like √(18x³)?”*

**Player:**
> *"Hanapin natin ang perfect square factors. 18 = 9 × 2, at x³ = x² × x. So √(18x³) = 3x√(2x). Practice tayo.”*

**Quiz prompt:**
> *Simplify the radical expression: √(50a⁵b²)*
> - Answer: 5a²b√(2a)

**After success:**
> *Carla:* “Kaya ko na pala! Sana pumasa ako bukas.”

## Mission 21: Scholarship Qualifier

**NPC:** Sarah (Scholarship applicant)  
**Location:** Provincial Capitol

**Sarah:**
> *"Kuya/Ate, kailangan ko pumasa sa scholarship exam. May radical equations daw. Hindi ko alam kung paano simulan.”*

**Player:**
> *"Radical equation? Isolate muna natin ang radical, then square both sides. Tandaan: i-check mo palagi ang answer, kasi minsan nagkakaroon ng extraneous solution.”*

**Sarah:**
> *"Extraneous solution? Ano yun?”*

**Player:**
> *"Yung answer na hindi pala totoo kapag isinubstitute mo sa original equation. Kaya dapat i-check.”*

**Quiz prompt:**
> *Solve for x: √(x + 5) = 4*
> - Answer: x = 11 (check: √16 = 4 ✓)

**After success:**
> *Sarah:* “Thank you po! Sana makapasa ako. Kapag nakapasa ako, sasama ko kayo sa program.”

## Mission 31: Regional Qualifier

**NPC:** Marco (Contestant)  
**Location:** Regional DepEd Office

**Marco:**
> *"Coach, kailangan ko ng edge sa regional competition. Ang radicals, kaya ko na, pero kapag may variables on both sides, nalilito ako.”*

**Player:**
> *"Dahan-dahan lang. Isolate the radical first. Then square both sides. Then solve. Kung may second radical pa, isolate ulit at square ulit.”*

**Marco:**
> *"So parang layers? Sunod-sunod lang?”*

**Player:**
> *"Exactly. Math is just one step at a time. Try this regional-level problem.”*

**Quiz prompt:**
> *Solve for x: √(2x + 3) = √(x + 7)*
> - Answer: x = 4

**After success:**
> *Marco:* “Ang linaw! Ready na ako sa qualifier.”

## Mission 41: Olympiad Radical Equations

**NPC:** Sofia (National trainee)  
**Location:** DepEd Central Office

**Sofia:**
> *"Kuya/Ate, Olympiad level na ito. Minsan may nested radicals o kaya naman radical sa loob ng fraction. Paano?”*

**Player:**
> *"Sa Olympiad, hindi lang basta solve — kailangan clever ka. Minsang rationalize mo muna ang denominator, o kaya i-express mo ang radical sa pinaka-simple form.”*

**Sofia:**
> *"Pakita mo nga sa halimbawa.”*

**Player:**
> *"Sure. Try this: solve √(x + 1) + √(x - 1) = 2. Isolate one radical, square, then isolate again.”*

**Quiz prompt:**
> *Solve for x: √(x + 1) + √(x - 1) = 2*
> - Answer: x = 5/4

**After success:**
> *Sofia:* “Grabe, ang galing ninyo magturo. Sana maging katulad ko kayo someday.”

## NPC Greeting Style Guide

Use these styles depending on NPC type:

| NPC Type | Greeting Style | Example |
|----------|----------------|---------|
| **Young student** | Shy or excited, uses "Kuya/Ate" | *"Kuya/Ate, tulungan niyo po ako...”* |
| **Parent** | Polite, practical | *"Anak, pwede mo bang turuan ang anak ko?”* |
| **Teacher / coach** | Professional, respectful | *"I heard you’re tutoring students in radicals.”* |
| **Admin / coordinator** | Formal but warm | *"We’d like to invite you to our program.”* |
| **Top student / contestant** | Confident but seeking edge | *"Coach, paano ko pa mapapabilis ang solving ko?”* |

## Quiz Dialogue Format

Each mission follows this flow:

1. **Greeting** — NPC explains their problem.
2. **Mini-lesson** — Player gives a short, relatable explanation.
3. **Quiz prompt** — Math question appears.
4. **Answer** — Player selects or types answer.
5. **Feedback** — Correct: praise + explanation. Wrong: gentle correction + hint.
6. **Reward** — Coins, points, and unlock next mission.

### Correct answer feedback example:
> *Player:* “Tama! Ang diagonal ay √1009, approximately 31.76 meters. Makikita mo, square root lang ang kailangan para makuha ang diagonal.”

### Wrong answer feedback example:
> *Player:* “Not quite. Tandaan, gamitin ang Pythagorean theorem: c = √(a² + b²). Subukan ulit natin.”
